## 1. Knowledge → understanding layer

Bridged has no Company Brain connected, so the engine started from uploaded documents (Intelligence → Knowledge).

| Document | Type | What the engine extracted |
|---|---|---|
| bridged-icp.md | icp | 7 ICP traits, 4 personas, 6 pain points, 3 objections, 3 competitors |
| bridged-case-studies.md | case_study | 8 proof points |
| bridged-product.pdf (2 p.) | product | 12 value props, 4 restrictions |
| bridged-brand-voice.md | brand | , 9 voice rules |
| bridged-email-samples.txt | email_samples | 3 reference emails, 5 voice rules |

**Ideal customer**
- Digital publishers with 1M+ monthly unique readers in the US, UK and India
- Media groups running several titles on one shared ad and content stack
- Publishers whose revenue mix is at least 40% advertising
- Teams that already run a newsletter or membership product and want more logged-in readers
- Recently relaunched a homepage, app or newsletter hub
- Hiring for audience, growth or revenue operations roles

**Personas**
- Head of Audience or Audience Development: owns engagement, retention and newsletter sign-ups
- Chief Revenue Officer or VP Ad Sales: owns ad yield, direct sales and sponsorship revenue
- Founder or CEO of an independent digital publisher: owns growth and the P&L end to end
- Head of Product or Digital: owns the reading experience and site performance

**Pain points**
- Readers leave after one article, so pages per session and time on site stay flat
- Programmatic CPMs keep falling and direct sales can't fill the gap on their own
- Newsletter and registration walls convert poorly and annoy loyal readers
- Sponsors ask for engagement proof that a banner impression can't give
- Editorial teams have no time to build interactive formats by hand
- First-party data is thin, so targeting gets harder every quarter as cookies disappear

**Value propositions**
- Bridged is an AI engagement layer for digital publishers.
- It turns every article into an interactive experience readers actually use, and turns that engagement into first-party data and new ad inventory.
- AI-generated interactive formats (quizzes, polls, explainers and "ask this article" Q&A) created automatically from each story
- Smart next-read journeys that keep readers moving through the site instead of bouncing
- Zero-party data capture: readers answer a question and become a known, consented profile
- Sponsored interactive units that sell at premium CPMs because sponsors get measurable engagement

**Proof points (cited in emails)**
- Northwind Daily: pages per session rose 38% within eight weeks of launch
- Northwind Daily: newsletter sign-ups from article pages grew 2.4x
- Northwind Daily: sponsored interactive units sold at a 3.1x CPM premium over standard display
- Contoso Media Group: time on site increased 27% across all seven titles
- Contoso Media Group: built a consented first-party audience of 410,000 readers in one quarter
- Contoso Media Group: direct-sold revenue grew 19% year over year, driven by sponsored quizzes

**Never say**
- Never promise a specific revenue uplift for a prospect without citing a named case study
- Don't call Bridged a chatbot
- Never claim we replace the editorial team
- Avoid hype words like revolutionary, game-changing or disruptive

**Voice profile** (learned from 3 sample emails): direct and conversational, ~67 words, opens "Hi", signs off "Best".
- Write like a thoughtful peer from the publishing industry, not a vendor
- Plain, specific English; short sentences; one idea per paragraph
- Lead with the reader's problem, not with Bridged
- Respect editors: we help newsrooms, we never replace them
- First-touch emails stay under 120 words
- One clear ask per email, phrased as a low-friction question
- No links, attachments or images in the first email
- Use the prospect's first name; no "Dear Sir/Madam"
