# Bridged: Ideal Customer Profile

## Ideal customer
- Digital publishers with 1M+ monthly unique readers in the US, UK and India
- Media groups running several titles on one shared ad and content stack
- Publishers whose revenue mix is at least 40% advertising
- Teams that already run a newsletter or membership product and want more logged-in readers

## Buyer personas
- Head of Audience or Audience Development: owns engagement, retention and newsletter sign-ups
- Chief Revenue Officer or VP Ad Sales: owns ad yield, direct sales and sponsorship revenue
- Founder or CEO of an independent digital publisher: owns growth and the P&L end to end
- Head of Product or Digital: owns the reading experience and site performance

## Pain points
- Readers leave after one article, so pages per session and time on site stay flat
- Programmatic CPMs keep falling and direct sales can't fill the gap on their own
- Newsletter and registration walls convert poorly and annoy loyal readers
- Sponsors ask for engagement proof that a banner impression can't give
- Editorial teams have no time to build interactive formats by hand
- First-party data is thin, so targeting gets harder every quarter as cookies disappear

## Buying signals
- Recently relaunched a homepage, app or newsletter hub
- Hiring for audience, growth or revenue operations roles
- Publicly talking about first-party data or reader revenue

## Objections
- We already have a recommendation widget: Bridged replaces dead-end widgets with formats readers interact with, and runs alongside existing tools
- Engineering has no bandwidth: setup is a single script tag and a two-week pilot on one section
- Worried about page speed: formats load after the article and are under 40 KB

## Competitors
- Taboola and Outbrain style recommendation widgets
- In-house interactive teams building quizzes and polls by hand
- Generic chatbot vendors
