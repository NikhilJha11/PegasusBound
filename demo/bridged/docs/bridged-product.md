# Bridged product overview

## What we do
Bridged is an AI engagement layer for digital publishers. It turns every article into an interactive experience readers actually use, and turns that engagement into first-party data and new ad inventory.

## Capabilities
- AI-generated interactive formats (quizzes, polls, explainers and "ask this article" Q&A) created automatically from each story
- Smart next-read journeys that keep readers moving through the site instead of bouncing
- Zero-party data capture: readers answer a question and become a known, consented profile
- Sponsored interactive units that sell at premium CPMs because sponsors get measurable engagement
- One script tag, no engineering sprint, and it works with your existing CMS and ad stack
- Editorial controls: every generated format can be reviewed, edited or switched off per section

## Value
- More pages per session and longer time on site
- More newsletter sign-ups and registrations without hard walls
- New premium ad inventory that is not dependent on third-party cookies
- First-party audience data your sales team can package for advertisers

## Approved claims
- Bridged works with the existing CMS and ad stack; no migration needed
- A pilot can go live on one section in about two weeks
- Formats load after the article content and do not block the page

## Do not say
- Never promise a specific revenue uplift for a prospect without citing a named case study
- Don't call Bridged a chatbot
- Never claim we replace the editorial team
- Avoid hype words like revolutionary, game-changing or disruptive
